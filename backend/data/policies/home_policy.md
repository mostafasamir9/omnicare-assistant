# Section 1: Coverage

## 1.1 Dwelling Coverage
Dwelling coverage protects the structure of your home up to the policy limit.
This includes the roof, walls, foundation, and permanently attached fixtures.

## 1.2 Personal Property
Personal property is covered up to 50% of the dwelling limit. This includes
furniture, electronics, clothing, and appliances.

## 1.3 Loss of Use
If your home becomes uninhabitable due to a covered loss, loss of use
coverage pays for temporary housing up to 20% of the dwelling limit.

# Section 2: Exclusions

## 2.1 Flood Damage
Flood damage is excluded from this policy and requires a separate flood
insurance policy, typically through the National Flood Insurance Program.

## 2.2 Earthquake Damage
Earthquake damage is excluded from this policy and requires a separate
earthquake policy or endorsement.

## 2.3 Neglect and Maintenance
Damage resulting from neglect, poor maintenance, or gradual deterioration is
excluded.

# Section 3: Limits

## 3.1 Jewelry and Valuables
Jewelry, watches, furs, and precious stones are covered up to $1,500 total
unless individually scheduled on the policy.

## 3.2 Electronics and Computers
Home electronics and computers are covered up to $2,500 total.

## 3.3 Deductible
The standard deductible is $1,000 per claim, or 2% of the dwelling limit for
wind and hail claims.
