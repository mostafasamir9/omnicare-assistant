# Section 1: Coverage

## 1.1 Collision Coverage
Collision coverage pays for damage to your vehicle caused by a collision with
another vehicle or object, up to the actual cash value (ACV), minus your
deductible. This applies regardless of fault.

## 1.2 Comprehensive Coverage
Comprehensive coverage pays for damage to your vehicle caused by events other
than a collision, including theft, fire, vandalism, weather events, and animal
strikes. Theft of the vehicle or its parts is covered under this section.

# Section 2: Exclusions

## 2.1 Racing and Track Use
Damage occurring during racing, speed contests, or on a racetrack is excluded
from all coverage under this policy.

## 2.2 Intentional Damage
Intentional damage caused by the policyholder or any household member is
excluded from all coverage.

## 2.3 Commercial Use
Damage occurring while the vehicle is used for commercial delivery or
ride-sharing is excluded unless a commercial endorsement is added.

# Section 3: Limits and Deductibles

## 3.1 Deductible
The standard deductible is $500 for collision coverage and $250 for
comprehensive coverage.

## 3.2 Rental Reimbursement
Rental reimbursement is covered up to $30 per day for a maximum of 30 days
following a covered loss.

## 3.3 Liability Limits
Bodily injury liability is $100,000 per person / $300,000 per accident.
Property damage liability is $50,000 per accident.
