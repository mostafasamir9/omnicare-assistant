# Section 1: Coverage

## 1.1 Death Benefit
The death benefit is paid to the named beneficiary upon the insured's death,
provided the policy is in force and premiums are current.

## 1.2 Accelerated Death Benefit
Up to 50% of the death benefit may be accelerated if the insured is
diagnosed with a terminal illness with a life expectancy of 12 months or
less.

## 1.3 Terminal Illness Rider
The terminal illness rider is included at no additional premium and provides
the accelerated benefit described in Section 1.2.

# Section 2: Exclusions

## 2.1 Suicide Clause
Death by suicide within two years of policy issue is excluded, and the
premiums paid will be refunded to the beneficiary.

## 2.2 Material Misrepresentation
Death resulting from material misrepresentation on the application may be
excluded if discovered within the contestability period.

## 2.3 War and Active Duty
Death resulting from war or active military duty in a declared conflict is
excluded unless a military rider is added.

# Section 3: Waiting Period

## 3.1 Contestability Period
The policy is contestable for two years from the issue date. After this
period, the insurer cannot contest the policy except for non-payment of
premiums.

## 3.2 Premium Grace Period
A 31-day grace period applies to premium payments. Coverage remains in force
during this period.
